About

I try to be sharp and highly personable, with curiosity, conviction, and enough humility to keep learning while still calling out what matters.

Professional intro

I’m Aditi Ghalsasi, currently a Senior Analyst at DeciBio, a strategy consulting firm focused on precision medicine and the broader life sciences ecosystem.

My academic journey started at the University of Pennsylvania, where I joined the Jerome Fisher Program in Management & Technology (M&T). I chose to study both Bioengineering and Finance and, as a self-identified “Big T,” spent many hours in the lab building with mRNA and thinking about how the technologies we worked on could one day become products and companies.

Today, I have the privilege of exploring the relationship between biology, technology, and capital—mapping where new platforms might wedge into existing workflows, how value accrues across the stack, and what it takes to turn good science into enduring businesses. I keep up with industry trends in stealth mode on X and write with friends on Substack and DeciBio’s newsletters.

Fun intro

I’ve been labeled the resident Excel-nerd for my love of a good, elegantly complex index-match. My mom is an expert in authentic Marathi cuisine, which is a rare commodity, and I call her regularly to master new recipes. I’m not a professional chef (or banker), but the hours I spend perfecting my Excel sheets and kitchen experiments are a pretty good proxy for how I approach everything: obsessive curiosity plus a desire to do things well.

Beyond the bullpen life, I try to live in the present. Whether it’s baking a three-tier cake for my first “Cake Picnic” or traveling to eight countries in two months, I’m usually chasing the next adventure—and the stories and people that come with it.

What I care about

The throughline across my work is a fascination with platforms and infrastructure in biology: mRNA and delivery, tools and diagnostics that make biology more programmable, and the long-overdue rebuilding of women’s health infrastructure. I’m especially drawn to founders who operate with scientific rigor, emotional intelligence, and a slightly unreasonable amount of persistence.

This site is a living document of that curiosity: essays I’ve written, companies I’m watching, and the path I’ve taken so far.
